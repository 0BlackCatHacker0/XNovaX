
==============================
THE COMPLETE CHESS MASTER BIBLE
==============================

SECTION 1 — WHAT CHESS REALLY IS
Chess is the control of future possibilities.
Resources:
Time (initiative, development)
Space (controlled squares)
Structure (pawn skeleton)

Material matters only when converting one of the above.

------------------------------
SECTION 2 — GAME PHASES
1 Opening – claim possibility
2 Middlegame – create imbalance
3 Conversion – turn advantage into inevitability
4 Endgame – geometry

------------------------------
SECTION 3 — OPENINGS
Choose a pawn structure → choose a future.

Hierarchy:
1 King safety
2 Activity
3 Center
4 Structure
5 Material

Common structures:
Open center: tactics
Closed center: maneuver
Semi-open: imbalance
Hypermodern: provoke
