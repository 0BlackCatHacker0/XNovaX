CHESS MOVE PATTERN MASTER FILE (All legal move *patterns*, not every move ever played)

Why: The set of all moves ever played in history is unbounded and not centrally recorded. This file enumerates the complete legal move *patterns* by piece and square, plus special moves (castling, en passant, promotion) and notation rules.

==============================================================================
RULES & NOTATION QUICK REFERENCE
==============================================================================
Coordinate squares: a1..h8 (file a-h, rank 1-8).

Piece letters in algebraic notation (SAN):
K=King, Q=Queen, R=Rook, B=Bishop, N=Knight, pawns have no letter.
Examples: Nf3, Qxe7, R1e1, Bb5+

Captures: "x" (e.g., Bxe6). Checks: "+"  Checkmate: "#".
Castling: O-O (king side), O-O-O (queen side).
Promotion: "=Q/R/B/N" (e.g., e8=Q).
En passant: pawn capture of a pawn that advanced two squares, on the immediately following move.

This file lists destination squares on an EMPTY board. Real legality also depends on:
- occupied squares (blocking for sliders: bishop/rook/queen)
- captures only on occupied enemy squares
- king safety (you cannot leave your king in check)

==============================================================================
SPECIAL MOVES
==============================================================================
CASTLING (FIDE rules summary)
- King and the castling rook have not moved.
- Squares between king and rook are empty.
- King is not currently in check.
- King does not pass through or land on a checked square.
- White: King e1 with rook h1 -> O-O moves king to g1 rook to f1.
         King e1 with rook a1 -> O-O-O moves king to c1 rook to d1.
- Black: King e8 with rook h8 -> O-O moves king to g8 rook to f8.
         King e8 with rook a8 -> O-O-O moves king to c8 rook to d8.

EN PASSANT
- If a pawn advances two squares from its starting rank and lands adjacent to an enemy pawn,
  that enemy pawn may capture it as if it moved one square (only on the immediately next move).

PROMOTION
- A pawn reaching the last rank must promote to Q, R, B, or N.
